JudyBid Analyze™ - Product Overview
What is JudyBid Analyze™?

JudyBid Analyze™ (also called JudyBid™) is a government bid review and opportunity matching assistant from NOFA AI Factory™. It helps small businesses find, review, and understand government bid opportunities before they spend time preparing a response.

JudyBid's tagline is: "JudyBid advises; the user decides."

How JudyBid works in one paragraph: the user enters a business profile (and can upload or paste a capability statement), then clicks "Find Opportunities Using My Business Profile". JudyBid searches connected live government sources using the profile, scores every returned opportunity for fit on a 0-100% scale, and shows why each opportunity looks relevant, possible concerns, and next steps for review.

Where to use JudyBid

JudyBid Analyze™ is a web application used in a browser at https://www.judybid.com/ . Nothing needs to be installed. A native mobile app is not part of the current product.

Who JudyBid is designed for

JudyBid is designed for small businesses that want to find and screen government bid opportunities (federal, state, local, and education) without reading every solicitation first. The business profile includes small-business set-aside choices such as Small Business, Woman-Owned (WOSB), Veteran-Owned, Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned (SDVOSB), SBA 8(a), HUBZone, Minority-Owned, and DBE. The Primary Industry list in the profile includes IT & Software, Healthcare, Construction, Professional Services, Education, and Security.

Business purpose and use case

Responding to a government solicitation takes significant time. JudyBid helps a business decide where to spend that time by showing which opportunities line up with its services, NAICS codes, certifications, service area, and other profile signals, and by pointing out risks such as an approaching deadline, a NAICS mismatch, or a set-aside requirement the profile does not reflect.

Current features

JudyBid currently includes:
- A business profile (name, industry, service area, NAICS codes, set-aside eligibility, PSC/FSC codes, NIGP codes, capability keywords, budget range, and contract types).
- Capability statement upload (PDF or TXT) or paste, with text extracted automatically for matching.
- Live opportunity search of federal opportunities from SAM.gov and of state, local, and education opportunities from connected SLED (state, local, and education) sources.
- Fit scoring from 0 to 100% with fit labels: Strong fit, Possible fit, Needs review, and Weak fit.
- Plain-language explanations for each result: "Why this looks relevant" and "Possible concerns", plus signal chips.
- Quick filters, sorting, a details view, copy buttons for solicitation number and title, and links to open the opportunity or its source portal.
- Export of match results as JSON or CSV.
- Saving an opportunity to your account (sign-in required).
- Email/password and Google sign-in, with password reset.
- A subscription (billed through Stripe) that unlocks live search.

What JudyBid does not do

To avoid confusion, JudyBid currently does not:
- Submit bids or proposals for you.
- Write proposals, compliance checklists, or response outlines.
- Provide legal advice, certify eligibility, or guarantee that a business will win an award.
- Download or read the full solicitation documents and attachments. JudyBid scores based on the listing information it receives (such as title or description, agency, NAICS code, set-aside, dates, and location).
- Send alerts or monitor for new opportunities automatically.
- Replace procurement, legal, financial, or compliance review.
