JudyBid Analyze™ - Getting Started and User Workflow
Step-by-step JudyBid workflow

1. Open JudyBid. Go to https://www.judybid.com/ in a web browser.
2. Create an account or sign in. Click "Sign In", then choose "Create Account" or "Sign In". You can continue with Google or use an email address and password. If you forget your password, click "Forgot password?" in the sign-in window, enter your email, and JudyBid sends a password reset email.
3. Subscribe to unlock live search. Click "Subscribe" (the monthly price shown in the app is $99/month) and complete Stripe Checkout. You can build your profile before subscribing, but live search requires an active subscription.
4. Build your business profile. Enter your Business Name, Primary Industry, Location / Service Area (for example "Gaithersburg, MD; Maryland, DC, Virginia"), NAICS codes separated by commas (for example 541511, 541512), your set-aside eligibility, and your capability keywords (for example "AI, chatbots, cloud, security"). NIGP codes, a budget range, and contract types are optional.
5. Add your capability statement. Paste the text into the Capability Statement box, or use "Upload Capability Statement" to choose a PDF or TXT file up to 5 MB. JudyBid reads the text in your browser and fills the box. Review the text and edit it if needed.
6. Choose an Opportunity Source. Select All Sources, Federal (SAM.gov), State / Local, Education, or Grants. If you are not sure, start with All Sources.
7. Click "Find Opportunities Using My Business Profile". JudyBid shows a status message such as "Found 12 federal and 8 state/local/education opportunities." and lists the results with fit scores. Each search uses one of your monthly live searches.
8. Review the results. Start with Strong fit and Possible fit results. Read "Why this looks relevant" and "Possible concerns", use Quick Filters or Sort by, open Details, then click Open (or Open Source Portal) to read the actual solicitation. For portal links, use "Copy #" or "Copy Title" to find the listing inside the portal.
9. Decide. JudyBid advises; the user decides. Verify deadlines, forms, addenda, certifications, and pricing in the official solicitation before you prepare a response. Use Export to download matches as JSON or CSV, or Save to store an opportunity in your account.

Tips for better results

- Enter your most important NAICS codes first. Only the first 5 NAICS codes are used to query live sources.
- Use specific capability keywords (for example "cybersecurity" or "web applications") rather than generic words. Generic words such as service, services, business, company, commercial, government, solution, solutions, and support are ignored.
- Write your service area with city and state names or two-letter abbreviations, and keep the field short. State and local results are limited to the states JudyBid can recognize in that field.
- Add a capability statement. It adds keywords, possible NAICS codes, and set-aside signals to your matching.
- Check due dates. Search results can include opportunities whose deadline has already passed.
- Use the Advanced Search Filters (keyword, NAICS, location, solicitation type) only to narrow the results you already have.

Typical result messages in JudyBid

- "Found N federal and M state/local/education opportunities." The search worked. N and M are counts from each source.
- "No matching opportunities found." The sources responded but nothing matched. Try broader keywords, check your NAICS codes, or switch the Opportunity Source.
- "(Federal search unavailable.)" or "(State/local search unavailable.)" One source did not respond. Results from the other source still appear. Try again later.
- "Sign in to search live government opportunities." You need to sign in first.
- "An active JudyBid subscription is required to search live government opportunities." Subscribe to unlock live search.
- "You've reached your monthly live-search limit. Your allowance resets on [date]." The 100 live searches for the billing period have been used.
- "Live search unavailable (...). Showing example opportunities." JudyBid could not complete the live search and is showing built-in example opportunities instead of real solicitations. Example opportunities are not real; they cannot be opened as live solicitations. Try the search again later.
