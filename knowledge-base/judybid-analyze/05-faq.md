JudyBid Analyze™ - Frequently Asked Questions
General questions about JudyBid

Q: What is JudyBid?
A: JudyBid Analyze™ (JudyBid™) is a government bid review and opportunity matching assistant from NOFA AI Factory™. It searches connected live government sources using your business profile, scores each opportunity for fit, and explains why it looks relevant and what to double-check. Web app: https://www.judybid.com/

Q: How does JudyBid find government contracts?
A: JudyBid uses your business profile (NAICS codes, capability keywords, service area, set-asides) and your optional capability statement to search connected live sources. Federal opportunities come from SAM.gov. State, local, and education opportunities come from connected SLED (state, local, and education) sources. JudyBid then scores the returned opportunities from 0 to 100% for fit and ranks them. It does not browse the whole internet or every agency website; it searches the connected sources.

Q: Who is JudyBid for? Is JudyBid right for my business?
A: JudyBid is designed for small businesses that want to find and screen government bid opportunities (federal, state, local, and education) before investing time in a response. It is a good fit if you want help finding relevant opportunities and seeing which ones match your NAICS codes, services, set-aside eligibility, and service area. It is not a proposal writer and does not submit bids, give legal advice, or guarantee awards. It works best when you know your NAICS codes and services, and it can only search what the connected sources provide. If you are unsure, the profile and capability statement can be built and saved before subscribing, and support can answer questions at supportdesk@nofabusinessconsulting.com.

Q: Does JudyBid find opportunities in every industry?
A: JudyBid matches on your NAICS codes and keywords, so it can be used for many service types. The Primary Industry list in the profile includes IT & Software, Healthcare, Construction, Professional Services, Education, and Security. The results you see depend on what is posted in the connected sources and how closely it matches your profile.

Q: Does JudyBid use AI?
A: JudyBid scores opportunities with rule-based fit scoring from your profile and capability statement. It is not an AI that writes bid analyses or reads full solicitation documents.

Questions about capability statements

Q: Can JudyBid analyze my capability statement?
A: Yes, in a specific way. You can paste it or upload a PDF or TXT file (up to 5 MB). JudyBid extracts the text in your browser and turns it into matching signals: keywords, possible NAICS codes (any six-digit numbers), set-aside terms such as small business, woman-owned, veteran-owned, and minority-owned/8(a), and state names. Those signals are used with your profile to score opportunities. JudyBid does not grade or rewrite the capability statement or give quality feedback on it.

Q: How do I upload my capability statement?
A: Sign in, open the Business Profile section, find "Capability Statement", and under "Upload Capability Statement" click the file chooser and select a PDF or TXT file (max 5 MB). JudyBid reads it and fills the "Paste Capability Statement" box, then shows "Capability statement extracted (N words). Ready for matching." You can also skip the upload and paste the text directly into the box.

Q: Why can't JudyBid read my PDF?
A: JudyBid reads selectable text only and does not use OCR. Scanned or image-only PDFs, password-protected files, and files over 5 MB cannot be read. Export the document as a text-based PDF or save it as TXT, or paste the text into the Capability Statement box. Word files (DOC/DOCX) are not supported for upload; copy and paste the text instead.

Q: Is my capability statement file stored by JudyBid?
A: The file itself is read in your browser and is not uploaded to a JudyBid server. The extracted text is saved as part of your business profile in your JudyBid account when you are signed in, and also in your browser. Do not include information in the text that you do not want saved with your profile.

Questions about searching

Q: How do users search for opportunities in JudyBid?
A: Complete the business profile, choose an Opportunity Source (All Sources, Federal, State / Local, Education, or Grants), and click "Find Opportunities Using My Business Profile". JudyBid searches the connected live sources and returns scored results. There is no free-text keyword box that replaces the profile; the optional Advanced Search Filters only narrow the results already returned.

Q: Does JudyBid search state and local opportunities?
A: Yes. Choose "State / Local" (or "All Sources") as the Opportunity Source. State, local, and education opportunities come from connected SLED sources, and JudyBid narrows them to the states it recognizes in your Location / Service Area field. Coverage depends on what the connected source provides, so JudyBid cannot promise that every state, city, county, school district, or procurement portal is included.

Q: Does JudyBid search federal opportunities?
A: Yes. Federal opportunities come from SAM.gov. JudyBid searches notices posted in the last 90 days, using the NAICS codes (first 5) and keywords from your profile.

Q: Why does a state or local result say "Open Source Portal" and not open the solicitation?
A: Some state and local sources only provide a procurement portal's home page rather than a direct link to the specific solicitation. JudyBid labels these "Open Source Portal". Use "Copy #" (solicitation number) or "Copy Title" and search for it inside that portal.

Q: How does JudyBid decide how well an opportunity matches me?
A: It scores each opportunity from 0 to 100% using capability keyword overlap, NAICS alignment, budget fit, set-aside fit, location fit, due-date urgency, and contract type. Labels are Strong fit (70%+), Possible fit (45-69%), Needs review (25-44%), and Weak fit (under 25%). Each result explains why it looks relevant and lists possible concerns.

Q: JudyBid isn't finding an opportunity I expected. Why?
A: Common reasons: the notice was posted more than 90 days ago (federal search covers the last 90 days); its NAICS code is not among the first 5 in your profile; your keywords are too generic, too few, or do not appear in the listing's title or description; the Opportunity Source selected does not include it (for example Federal vs State / Local); the state is not recognized in your service area field; the connected source does not carry that agency or portal; or the opportunity was returned but scored low and appears further down the list. Try All Sources, add the right NAICS code and specific keywords, and check lower-scored results. If it is still missing, reply with the solicitation number or link, your NAICS codes and keywords, your service area, and which Opportunity Source you used, and the NOFA support team can review it.

Q: Does JudyBid show opportunities that have already closed?
A: Possibly. Search results can include opportunities whose response deadline has passed. JudyBid flags this under Possible concerns ("Deadline may have passed"). Always verify the deadline in the official solicitation.

Q: What does it mean when JudyBid shows "example opportunities"?
A: If the live search cannot complete, JudyBid may display built-in example opportunities labeled as examples. They are not real solicitations. Run the search again later.

Q: Can JudyBid analyze the full RFP or solicitation document?
A: Not currently. JudyBid scores based on the listing information it receives (title or description, agency, NAICS code, set-aside, dates, and location). Read the full solicitation yourself and verify requirements before bidding.

Q: Can JudyBid submit a bid or write a proposal for me?
A: No. JudyBid does not submit bids or write proposals. JudyBid advises; the user decides.

Q: Does JudyBid send alerts for new opportunities?
A: Not currently. JudyBid Watch™ is a possible future path and is not available.

Account, pricing, and billing

Q: How much does JudyBid cost?
A: The JudyBid subscription shown in the app is $99 per month, billed through Stripe, and includes up to 100 live searches per monthly billing period. No free trial, annual plan, or other tiers are currently documented.

Q: Do I need an account?
A: Yes, to search live opportunities you must sign in with an active subscription. You can sign in with Google or with email and password. Use "Forgot password?" to reset a password.

Q: How do I cancel, or get a refund or an invoice?
A: The NOFA support team handles cancellations, refunds, invoices, and other account-specific billing requests. Email supportdesk@nofabusinessconsulting.com from the address on your account. JudyBid does not have a self-service billing portal and no refund policy is currently documented.

Q: What happens when I reach 100 live searches?
A: JudyBid shows that you reached the monthly live-search limit and the date your allowance resets, which is tied to your subscription's billing period.

Q: Can I export my results?
A: Yes. Use Export to download matches as JSON or CSV.
