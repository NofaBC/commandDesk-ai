JudyBid Analyze™ - Pricing and Subscription
JudyBid subscription price

JudyBid Analyze™ live search requires an active JudyBid subscription. The subscription price shown in the JudyBid app is $99 per month ("Subscribe for $99/month"). It is a monthly subscription paid by card through Stripe Checkout.

What the JudyBid subscription includes

The JudyBid subscription (referred to in the product as the Standard plan) unlocks live government opportunity search across all sources (federal SAM.gov, plus state, local, and education sources) and includes up to 100 live searches per monthly billing period.

How JudyBid live searches are counted

- One click of "Find Opportunities Using My Business Profile" counts as one live search, no matter how many NAICS codes or sources JudyBid checks behind the scenes.
- A search that completes but returns zero matching opportunities still counts as one search.
- If every connected source fails with a technical error, so that no live data is returned, the search is not counted against your allowance.
- The app shows "Live searches used: X of 100" after you run a search.
- When the 100-search allowance is reached, JudyBid shows "You've reached your monthly live-search limit" together with the date the allowance resets. The allowance resets when your subscription renews for the next billing period.

How to subscribe

1. Open https://www.judybid.com/ and sign in or create an account.
2. Click the Subscribe button in the header (or the Subscribe button shown in the results panel).
3. Complete payment on the secure Stripe Checkout page.
4. You are returned to JudyBid and your subscription is activated.

The subscription is attached to the JudyBid account you were signed in with when you subscribed. If you sign in later with a different account, for example Google sign-in versus email and password, that account will not have the subscription.

What you can do without a subscription

You can create an account, sign in, build and save your business profile, and upload or paste your capability statement. Live opportunity search (the "Find Opportunities Using My Business Profile" button) requires an active subscription. Without one, JudyBid shows: "An active JudyBid subscription is required to search live government opportunities."

Payment problems and access status

- If a renewal payment fails, Stripe retries the payment. While the subscription is marked past due, JudyBid keeps live search access available during the retry period.
- If the subscription is fully canceled or ends because payment could not be collected, live search access ends.
- If you just subscribed and live search still says a subscription is required, make sure you are signed in to the same account you subscribed with, refresh the page, and sign out and back in. If it still does not unlock, email supportdesk@nofabusinessconsulting.com with the email address on your JudyBid account.

Billing questions that support handles

Cancellations, refunds, invoices and receipts, changing payment details, and other account-specific billing requests are handled by the NOFA support team at supportdesk@nofabusinessconsulting.com. The JudyBid app does not currently have a billing dashboard or account portal.

Not defined (do not state or imply these exist)

The following are not defined in JudyBid's current documentation or implementation, so they must not be promised: a free trial, an annual plan, additional plan tiers, discounts or coupons, a refund policy, a money-back guarantee, a higher or unlimited search allowance, or team and multi-user pricing. If a customer asks about any of these, say that no such option is currently documented and direct them to supportdesk@nofabusinessconsulting.com.
