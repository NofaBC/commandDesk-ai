JudyBid Analyze™ - Troubleshooting, Limitations, and Support Procedure
Troubleshooting common JudyBid problems

Problem: JudyBid says "Sign in to search live government opportunities."
Fix: Click Sign In and sign in or create an account. Live search requires a signed-in account with an active subscription.

Problem: JudyBid says "An active JudyBid subscription is required to search live government opportunities", even though I subscribed.
Fix: The subscription belongs to the account you were signed in with when you subscribed. Confirm you are signed in with that same account (for example the same Google account or the same email and password). Refresh the page, then sign out and sign back in. Activation can take a short time after Stripe Checkout. If it still does not unlock, email supportdesk@nofabusinessconsulting.com with the email on your JudyBid account and the approximate time you subscribed. This may need a technical review.

Problem: JudyBid says "You've reached your monthly live-search limit."
Cause: All 100 live searches in the current billing period have been used. The allowance resets on the date shown, when the subscription renews. Contact support at supportdesk@nofabusinessconsulting.com if the date or count looks wrong.

Problem: "No matching opportunities found."
Fix: This means the connected sources responded but nothing matched. Check that your NAICS codes are correct and listed in order of importance (only the first 5 are used), use more specific capability keywords, avoid generic words (service, business, company, government, solution, support are ignored), and try "All Sources". Remember that federal search covers notices posted in the last 90 days.

Problem: "(Federal search unavailable.)" or "(State/local search unavailable.)"
Cause: One connected source did not respond. Results from the other source still appear, and a search where every source fails is not counted against your allowance. Wait a few minutes and try again. If it keeps happening for more than a short time, report it to support.

Problem: "Live search unavailable (...). Showing example opportunities."
Cause: The live search could not complete. The opportunities shown are built-in examples, not real solicitations. Try again later. If it persists, report it to support with the full message shown.

Problem: Capability statement upload fails: "Could not read this file. Paste the capability statement text instead."
Fix: The PDF is probably scanned or image-only, or the file has no selectable text. Save it as a text-based PDF or TXT, or paste the text into the box. Files must be PDF or TXT and no larger than 5 MB. If the PDF reader is blocked by a browser extension, ad blocker, or network restriction, try another browser or disable the blocker for the JudyBid site. Other messages: "File too large (max 5 MB). Paste the text instead." and "Unsupported file type. Upload a PDF or TXT file."

Problem: Few or no state and local results.
Fix: Choose "State / Local" or "All Sources". Put your states in the Location / Service Area field using state names or two-letter abbreviations, and keep the field short, because short words such as "in", "or", "me", or "hi" can be read as state abbreviations. State and local data comes from a connected source and does not cover every agency or portal. Those listings usually have only a title, so specific keywords in your profile matter.

Problem: A state or local link goes to a general portal page.
Fix: This is expected for some portals. Use "Copy #" or "Copy Title" and search for the solicitation inside the portal.

Problem: The Advanced Search Filters did not bring up new results.
Cause: Keyword, NAICS, location, and solicitation type overrides only narrow the results already returned. To broaden the search, change the profile (NAICS codes, capability keywords, service area) or the Opportunity Source and search again.

Problem: Grants shows no results.
Fix: JudyBid is mainly a bid and solicitation tool and Grants results can be limited. Try Federal or All Sources.

Problem: An opportunity scored low or "Weak fit" but looks relevant.
Cause: Scoring is rule-based. A result scores low when its title or description does not share your keywords, its NAICS code does not match, or it carries set-aside requirements not in your profile. Add specific keywords and NAICS codes, add a capability statement, and review lower-ranked results manually.

Problem: Page does not load, buttons do not respond, or data does not save.
Fix: Refresh, try another modern browser, disable extensions that block scripts, and check your connection. JudyBid loads its sign-in, data, and PDF tools from external services, so blocked scripts can break features. If the problem continues, report it to support.

Known limitations of JudyBid

- Search covers the connected sources only: SAM.gov for federal and a connected SLED source for state, local, and education. Coverage of every agency or portal is not guaranteed.
- Federal search looks at notices posted in the last 90 days, uses up to 5 NAICS codes, and uses a limited number of keywords.
- Results may include opportunities that are already past their deadline.
- Scoring uses listing information, not full solicitation documents or attachments.
- State and local listings usually lack descriptions, NAICS codes, and budgets.
- PSC / FSC codes are collected but not currently used for search or scoring.
- Capability statement files must be PDF or TXT with selectable text, up to 5 MB.
- No bid submission, proposal writing, alerts, or automatic monitoring. JudyBid Watch™ is not available.
- No dedicated saved-opportunities page and no self-service billing portal in the current interface.

Support procedure

1. For how-it-works, feature, pricing, and troubleshooting questions, answer from this documentation.
2. For anything not covered here, do not guess. Say the detail is not available and direct the customer to supportdesk@nofabusinessconsulting.com.
3. Real technical defects go through NOFA's technical support escalation, not through guesses. Escalate when a customer reports an error message, a page that will not load, sign-in or password reset that fails, a checkout or subscription that does not activate, a live-search counter that looks wrong, data not saving, or repeated live-search failures. Customers receive a case reference number for escalated technical issues and can reply to the same email to continue the conversation.
4. Cancellations, refunds, invoices, and billing changes are handled by the NOFA support team by email.
5. When a customer says an expected opportunity is missing, explain the possible causes, then ask for: the solicitation number or link, the NAICS codes and keywords in the profile, the service area, the Opportunity Source selected, and the approximate date of the search.
6. Never promise awards, eligibility, compliance, legal advice, refunds, free trials, additional plans, or features that are not listed here. Never describe JudyBid Watch™ as available. Do not discuss internal or testing access.
