General Questions

What is NOFA AI Factory™?

NOFA AI Factory™ is a structured AI creation system that moves ideas from concept to working product. It combines strategy, AI orchestration, product design, rapid prototyping, deployment, and validation into one repeatable production process. It is operated by NOFA Business Consulting LLC and founded by Farhad Nasserghodsi.

Is NOFA AI Factory™ a single product?

No. NOFA AI Factory™ is a production system, not a single product. It has produced a portfolio of public SaaS products and internal AI engines, including Dlyn AI™, IntelliScan AI™, Magazinify AI™, TechSupport AI™, CommandDesk AI™, and more.

Who runs NOFA AI Factory™?

NOFA AI Factory™ is operated by NOFA Business Consulting LLC. The founder is Farhad Nasserghodsi, who specializes in AI orchestration and turning ideas into real AI tools and SaaS products.

The Assembly Line Process

How does NOFA AI Factory™ build products?

The Factory uses a 7-stage process called the NOFA AI Factory Assembly Line™:
1. Idea Discovery — Identify real-world pain and demand
2. Value Testing — Score the idea for usefulness, timing, and monetization
3. Product Blueprint — Define the MVP, features, and tech stack
4. AI Build — Build the product brick by brick
5. Deployment — Launch through GitHub, Vercel, Firebase, and Stripe
6. Validation — Test with real users and refine
7. Growth and Spinout — Scale and potentially spin into standalone companies

Why does the Factory use this process?

The Assembly Line ensures that only ideas with real value get built, and that those ideas are built correctly and shipped to market rather than staying as concepts.
