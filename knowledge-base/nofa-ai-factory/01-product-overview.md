NOFA AI Factory™ - Product Overview
What is NOFA AI Factory™?

NOFA AI Factory™ is a structured AI creation system designed to move ideas from concept to working product. It combines strategy, AI orchestration, product design, rapid prototyping, deployment, and validation into one repeatable process.

NOFA AI Factory™ is not just one product — it is a full production system operated by NOFA Business Consulting LLC and founded by Farhad Nasserghodsi.

The Factory is responsible for building, shipping, and maintaining a growing portfolio of AI tools, SaaS products, internal support systems, and business automation solutions.
