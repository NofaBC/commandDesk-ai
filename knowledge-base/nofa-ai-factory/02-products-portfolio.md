NOFA AI Factory™ - Products Portfolio
Overview

NOFA AI Factory™ has produced a growing portfolio of public SaaS products and internal AI engines. These are early public examples of a much larger factory ecosystem.

Public SaaS Products

Dlyn AI™
Status: Live on Stripe

Dlyn AI™ is an AI-powered job search and career assistant. It helps professionals navigate career transitions with intelligent resume analysis, job matching, and interview preparation.

Previously known within the ecosystem as CareerPilot AI™.

IntelliScan AI™
Status: Live and Active

IntelliScan AI™ is an AI website scanner that analyzes a business website and recommends AI tools, vendors, or custom NOFA AI Factory solutions. It provides instant intelligence about a company's digital presence and how AI can improve it.

Magazinify AI™
Status: Live on Stripe

Magazinify AI™ is an AI-powered branding and visibility tool that helps businesses turn ideas into polished articles and magazine-style content. It delivers content creation at factory speed.

Internal Factory Engines

TechSupport AI™
Status: Internal Use

TechSupport AI™ is an internal AI support system for handling customer support, troubleshooting, knowledge base answers, and escalation. It keeps users supported while the factory continues building.

CommandDesk AI™
Status: Internal Use

CommandDesk AI™ is an AI support command center for inbox triage, message classification, and customer response management. It organizes communication at scale across all NOFA AI Factory products.

CommandDesk AI™ serves as the official email intelligence, customer communication, knowledge-retrieval, and support-routing platform for NOFA AI Factory™.

NOFA Command Core™
Status: In Development

NOFA Command Core™ is the future central command system for monitoring all SaaS products, revenue, expenses, alerts, support activity, product priorities, and growth signals. It is designed to be the brain of the factory.

NOFA Priority Engine™
Status: Planning Stage

NOFA Priority Engine™ is a decision engine that helps score and rank which AI product should be built next. It removes guesswork from the production pipeline.

Ecosystem Architecture

The NOFA AI Factory ecosystem uses a clean architecture separation:

CommandDesk AI™: Customer communication, email automation, knowledge retrieval, and routing.
TechSupport AI™: Technical case management, human collaboration, escalation, and issue resolution.

This architecture allows the Factory to continue expanding without redesigning the support system each time a new AI product is launched.
