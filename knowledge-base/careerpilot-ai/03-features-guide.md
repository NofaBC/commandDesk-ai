# CareerPilot AI - Features Guide

## AI Career Assessment

### What It Does
Analyzes your skills, experience, interests, and goals to provide personalized career recommendations.

### How It Works
1. Complete the 15-minute assessment questionnaire
2. AI analyzes your responses against market data
3. Receive personalized career matches with confidence scores
4. Get actionable recommendations for career development

### Available In
- Free Plan: Basic assessment
- Starter & Above: Advanced assessment with detailed insights

## Cover Letter Generator

### Personalized Cover Letters in Minutes
AI creates tailored cover letters for each application.

### How It Works
1. Upload job description or paste job URL
2. Select which resume to reference
3. AI generates customized cover letter
4. Edit and refine with AI suggestions
5. Export and send

### Customization Options
- Tone adjustment (formal, conversational, enthusiastic)
- Length control (concise, standard, detailed)
- Focus areas (skills, experience, culture fit)
- Company research integration

### Available In
- Free Plan: ❌ Not available
- Starter & Above: ✅ Available
