## Features & Functionality

### How does the AI career assessment work?
The assessment analyzes your skills, interests, values, and goals through a series of questions. Our AI compares your profile against market data and career paths to provide personalized recommendations.

### Can I upload my existing resume?
Yes! The resume builder can import existing resumes in PDF or DOCX format. The AI will extract and organize the content, then suggest improvements.

### How many resumes can I create?
- **Free**: 1 resume
- **Starter**: 3 resumes
- **Pro**: Unlimited resumes
- **Enterprise**: Unlimited resumes

### What is ATS optimization?
ATS (Applicant Tracking System) optimization ensures your resume can be properly read by the software many companies use to screen applications. Our AI checks format, keywords, and structure to maximize your chances of passing ATS filters.

### Does the interview simulator really help?
Yes! Research shows that practice significantly improves interview performance. Our AI simulator provides realistic questions, instant feedback, and tracks your improvement over time. Pro plan users can also practice with video for more comprehensive feedback.

### How accurate is the salary data?
Our salary data is sourced from multiple verified databases and updated regularly. We provide ranges based on location, experience, industry, and company size. While we strive for accuracy, actual salaries may vary.

### Can the AI really coach me on career decisions?
Yes! Our AI is trained on career coaching best practices and updated with current market data. While it's not a replacement for human career counseling in complex situations, it provides valuable guidance for most common career questions and is available 24/7.

### How does job tracking work?
Add jobs you're applying to, and CareerPilot AI helps you track:
- Application status and dates
- Interview schedules
- Follow-up reminders
- Contact information
- Related documents
- Success metrics

### Can I integrate with other tools?
Yes! We offer integrations with:
- Google Calendar and Outlook (interview scheduling)
- LinkedIn (profile optimization)
- Email (application tracking)
- Enterprise plans also include API access for custom integrations

## Resume & Applications

### How long should my resume be?
**General rule**: 1 page per 10 years of experience
- Entry-level: 1 page
- Mid-career: 1-2 pages
- Senior/Executive: 2-3 pages

Our AI will suggest the optimal length based on your experience.

### Should I use a creative or traditional template?
**Creative industries** (design, marketing, media): Creative templates okay
**Traditional industries** (finance, law, healthcare): Stick to Pro templates
**Tech/Startup**: Moderate creativity acceptable

When in doubt, choose a clean, ATS-friendly template.

### How do I tailor my resume for different jobs?
1. Create a master resume with all experience
2. For each application, create a targeted version
3. Use our "Job Match" feature to highlight relevant skills
4. Adjust summary and keywords based on job description
5. Pro plan users can create unlimited tailored versions

### What file format should I use?
**PDF** is recommended for most applications:
- Preserves formatting
- Works on any device
- Looks Pro

Use **DOCX** only if specifically requested by the employer.

## Common Issues

### Why am I not receiving emails?
Check:
1. Spam/junk folder
2. Email filters and rules
3. Add nofabusinessconsulting.com to your safe senders list
4. Verify email address in account settings

### My ATS score is low. What should I do?
Common fixes:
- Use standard section headings (Experience, Education, Skills)
- Avoid tables, images, or complex formatting
- Include relevant keywords from job description
- Use standard fonts (Arial, Calibri, Times New Roman)
- Save as PDF (not image file)
