CareerPilot AI - Frequently Asked Questions
General Questions
What is CareerPilot AI?

CareerPilot AI is an AI-powered job search command center that helps users build a profile, search for matched jobs, generate AI cover letters, track applications, create resumes, and practice interviews.

Who is CareerPilot AI for?

CareerPilot AI is designed for:

active job seekers

career changers

mid-career professionals

users running a structured job search campaign

What makes CareerPilot AI different?

It combines multiple job-search tools in one place:

profile builder

AI job matching

Smart Apply

resume PDF generation

interview coach

application tracking

Pricing & Credits

Yes. New users can begin with a 7-day free trial.

No credit card is required to start the trial.

Free

How do credits work?

AI-powered actions use credits:

Job Search = 8 credits

AI Cover Letter = 15 credits

Interview Practice = 25 credits

Resume Builder = free

Do subscription credits roll over?

No. Subscription credits reset monthly and do not roll over.

Do top-up credits expire?

No. Top-up credits never expire.

What happens when I run out of credits?

You can wait for your monthly credits to reset or purchase a top-up pack.
